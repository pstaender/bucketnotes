Just write.

A quiet Markdown editor that keeps every note as a plain file in your own S3 bucket. No account, no server in between, no tracking.

Start writing now

Free and open source · Runs in your browser · Works offline

  • Your bucket

    Notes are plain Markdown files in S3 storage you own. Keep versioning on and every draft stays recoverable.

  • Nothing in the way

    CommonMark, auto-save and image upload in one uncluttered surface. Link notes together zettelkasten-style.

  • Anywhere you are

    Install it as an app on desktop, tablet or phone and keep writing while you are offline.
